About Me

Hello! My name is Yaoge Yu, and I am currently studying Data Science and Economics at Northeastern University in Boston. I enjoy exploring how technology, statistics, and economic thinking can help us understand the world around us. Combining these two subjects allows me to approach questions from both a technical and a social science perspective.

Outside of academics, I enjoy playing tennis, listening to music, exploring new places, and meeting people from different backgrounds. Living and studying in Boston has given me the chance to experience a new environment and become more independent. In the future, I hope to continue improving my technical skills, work on interesting projects, and explore career opportunities involving data and finance.
