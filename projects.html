Exploring Data Science

As a Data Science and Economics student at Northeastern University, I am interested in understanding how data can help us answer meaningful questions. Through my coursework, I have learned about programming, statistical methods, database systems, and data visualization. I enjoy working with datasets because they allow me to explore patterns that are not always obvious from looking at individual observations.

My interest in economics also influences the way I approach technical projects. Economic theories provide a useful background for understanding incentives, markets, and human behavior. Combining data science with economics helps me think about both the numerical results of an analysis and their real-world meaning.

Areas I Want to Explore

  • Data visualization and interactive dashboards
  • Statistical analysis using Python
  • SQL and relational databases
  • Economics and financial markets
